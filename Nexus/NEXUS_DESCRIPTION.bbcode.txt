[b]Path Finder - 1.0.2[/b]
See a node's immediate input and output connections at a glance.
[url=https://github.com/urakkaamyx/KingdomInc_PathFinder/releases/latest]GitHub downloads[/url] | [url=https://github.com/urakkaamyx/KingdomInc_PathFinder]Source and bug reports[/url]

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Hover a node to highlight its direct parents' and direct children's pipes.
[*]One solid bright neon color per line, chosen from a rainbow palette, with a glow.
[*]A tiny pin at each node's top-left locks those highlights until clicked again.
[*]Bronze bevels, burgundy enamel, a tilted gold tack and cyan locked-state glow.
[*]Multiple pins work together while you hover another node.
[*]Follows bent pipes, mirrored endpoints, and rewiring.
[*]Shared straight sections separate into parallel lanes so overlapping colors stay visible.
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]
Kingdom Inc Unity Mono build. Tested on Release v1.0.10, Unity 6000.3.27f2.
BepInEx 5, tested with 5.4.23.6. No other mod dependency.
[url=https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx]BepInEx loader[/url]

[b]Installation / updating[/b]
Close the game and extract the player ZIP into its directory.
Expected file: BepInEx/plugins/PathFinder/PathFinder.dll
Restart, then hover a connected node or click its tiny pin. Replace this DLL for updates.

[b]Usage[/b]
Only immediate connections are highlighted. Each line has a single neon color.
Click the top-left pin to keep paths visible; cyan means pinned. Click again to unpin.
Pins last for the loaded kingdom session and are not saved. Eight colors repeat when needed.
Settings: BepInEx/config/urakka.kingdominc.pathfinder.cfg (Enabled and Width).

[b]Compatibility and known limits[/b]
Isolated tests cover native nodes with actual mouse/raycast input, rewiring, pinning, loops, and cleanup.
The companion test loads PartyNode, SharableBlueprints, KingdomFactoryApi and LedgerMod with the native test graph; it does not retest every companion feature or custom-node layout.
Manual installation supported. Vortex deployment untested.

[b]Removal[/b]
Close the game and remove BepInEx/plugins/PathFinder. No save cleanup needed.

[b]Changes in 1.0.2[/b]
Overlapping straight pipes now keep their individual solid colors visible in parallel highlight lanes. Native pipe geometry and saved bends remain unchanged.
