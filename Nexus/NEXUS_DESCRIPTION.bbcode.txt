[b]Path Finder - 1.1.0[/b]
See a node's immediate input and output paths at a glance.
[url=https://github.com/urakkaamyx/KingdomInc_PathFinder]Source and bug reports[/url]

[b]Unlock[/b]
Research Path Finder for 100 gold under Tech Tree → Mods to enable highlights and pins in that kingdom. The unlock is saved with your research progress.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Hover a node to highlight its direct input and output pipes.[/*]
[*]One solid bright neon color per line, with a soft glow.[/*]
[*]Tiny top-left pins keep paths highlighted after moving away.[/*]
[*]Bronze pins gain a cyan rim when pinned. Click again to unpin.[/*]
[*]Multiple pins and hover work together without duplicating shared pipes.[/*]
[*]Highlights follow bends and mirrored ports.[/*]
[*]Overlapping straight sections separate into parallel colored lanes so each line stays visible.[/*]
[/list]

[b]Requirements[/b]
Kingdom Inc, Windows Mono build.
BepInEx 5 for Windows x64.
Foundation 0.5.0 or later, installed separately.

[b]Installation / updating[/b]
Close the game and extract the player ZIP into the folder containing KingdomInc.exe.
Expected file: BepInEx/plugins/PathFinder/PathFinder.dll
Launch, open Tech Tree → Mods, and research Path Finder. Hover a connected node or click its tiny pin.
To update, close the game and replace the DLL.

[b]Usage and settings[/b]
Only immediate connections are highlighted. Pins last for the loaded kingdom session and are not saved.
Eight neon colors repeat when needed. Original pipes and saved bends stay in place.
Settings: BepInEx/config/urakka.kingdominc.pathfinder.cfg
Enabled: turn highlights on or off after research.
Width: solid line width from 2 to 12 world units; default 5.

[b]Removal[/b]
Close the game and remove BepInEx/plugins/PathFinder. Pins and highlights add no placed nodes or items.
